    Washington's oldest saloon, open since 1856, directly across from the White House. Dark wood, antique fixtures, raw bar. Reserve this one — it fills every night.

    675 15th St NW, Washington DC (map) Call 202-347-4800 ~$110
  10. 7:00 PMSIGHT

    7:00 PM: The monuments after dark

    Lincoln, Vietnam, Korean War, WWII, MLK, Jefferson. All outdoors, all lit, all open around the clock, and nearly empty on a November weeknight. This is the best thing on the whole route.

    Lincoln to Jefferson and back is about three miles on foot. If the legs or the weather give out, fetch the car and drive between them instead: there's free street parking along Ohio Drive by the Tidal Basin and near the Lincoln after 6:30 p.m.

    Lincoln Memorial (map) Jefferson Memorial (map) Ohio Dr parking (map) Free · 24 hrs

    Cobblestone Delaware Street, the Green, and Battery Park along the river. A colonial river town that stayed largely intact. Forty-five minutes on foot covers it, and it beats downtown Wilmington by a mile.

Why DC comes first

Washington sits west of the Chesapeake; Delaware, New Jersey and Philadelphia all sit on one line northeast of it. Hitting Delaware before DC would cost four hours of backtracking, and running DC as a day trip from Philly would cost five. The order is set by the map, not by preference.

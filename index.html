The monuments after dark

Lincoln, Vietnam, Korean War, WWII, MLK, Jefferson. All outdoors, all lit, all open around the clock, and nearly empty on a November weeknight. This is the best thing on the whole route.

Lincoln to Jefferson and back is about three miles on foot. If the legs or the weather give out, there's free street parking along Ohio Drive by the Tidal Basin and near the Lincoln after 6:30 p.m. — drive between them instead.

Why DC comes first Washington sits west of the Chesapeake; Delaware, New Jersey and Philadelphia all sit on one line northeast of it. Hitting Delaware before DC would cost four hours of backtracking, and running DC as a day trip from Philly would cost five. The order is set by the map, not by preference.

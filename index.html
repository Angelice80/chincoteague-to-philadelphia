Tuesday, November 3

Chincoteague → Annapolis → DC → Baltimore · 265 mi
  1. 6:30 AM

    6:30 AM: Breakfast — Mr. Baldy's Family Restaurant

    Opens at 6 a.m., the only real kitchen running that early on the island. Big portions, island prices.

    3441 Ridge Rd, Chincoteague VA ↗ (map) ~$25
  2. 7:15 AM
    Drive 2h 45m — US-13 north up the Delmarva, then US-50 west across the Chesapeake Bay Bridge ↗ (map). Westbound is free; the toll is eastbound only.
  3. 10:00 AM

    10:00 AM: Maryland State House, Annapolis

    Oldest state capitol still in legislative use, and the room where Washington resigned his commission. Largest wooden dome in the country. Bring ID to get in.

    100 State Cir, Annapolis MD ↗ (map) Free · 9–5 $6 parking
  4. 10:50 AM

    10:50 AM: Walk down Main Street to the harbor

    Ten minutes downhill from the State House to City Dock. Naval Academy gate is right there if you want to look.

    Free
  5. 11:30 AM

    11:30 AM: Lunch — Boatyard Bar & Grill

    Jumbo lump crab cakes are the reason to go. Usually a 40-minute wait; midweek in November there won't be one.

    400 Fourth St, Annapolis MD ↗ (map) ~$70
  6. 1:00 PM
    Drive 45m — US-50 west into DC. Leaving by 1:00 beats the afternoon wall on this road.
  7. 2:00 PM

    2:00 PM: Park once, near the Mall

    One garage in Penn Quarter or near the Mall. Leave the car there through dinner; everything until 7:00 is on foot.

    Penn Quarter garages ↗ (map) $25–40
  8. 2:15 PM

    2:15 PM: One Smithsonian — pick a single museum

    Air and Space or American History. All free, all close at 5:30. Two in an afternoon means seeing neither.

    Air and Space ↗ (map) American History ↗ (map) Free
  9. 5:00 PMReserved

    5:00 PM, reserved: Dinner — Old Ebbitt Grill

    Washington's oldest saloon, open since 1856, directly across from the White House. Dark wood, antique fixtures, raw bar. Reserve this one — it fills every night.

    675 15th St NW, Washington DC ↗ (map) Call 202-347-4800 ~$110
  10. 7:00 PM

    7:00 PM: The monuments after dark

    Lincoln, Vietnam, Korean War, WWII, MLK, Jefferson. All outdoors, all lit, all open around the clock, and nearly empty on a November weeknight. This is the best thing on the whole route.

    Lincoln to Jefferson and back is about three miles on foot. If the legs or the weather give out, fetch the car and drive between them instead: there's free street parking along Ohio Drive by the Tidal Basin and near the Lincoln after 6:30 p.m.

    Lincoln Memorial ↗ (map) Jefferson Memorial ↗ (map) Ohio Dr parking ↗ (map) Free · 24 hrs
  11. 9:45 PM
    Drive 1h — I-95 north to Baltimore. Sleep in Fells Point or Inner Harbor. Cheaper than DC, and it takes 45 minutes off tomorrow. Hotel: not booked yet

Wednesday, November 4

Baltimore → New Castle → Camden → Philadelphia · 135 mi
  1. 8:00 AM

    8:00 AM: Breakfast — Blue Moon Cafe, Fells Point

    Opens at 7. The Cap'n Crunch French toast is what people drive in for. Small room, so go early rather than at nine.

    1621 Aliceanna St, Baltimore MD ↗ (map) ~$40
  2. 9:30 AM
    Drive 2h — I-95 north. Northbound out of the DC–Baltimore corridor clears up by 9:30, which is why breakfast is unhurried.
  3. 11:45 AM

    11:45 AM: Historic New Castle, Delaware

    Cobblestone Delaware Street, the Green, and Battery Park along the river. A colonial river town that stayed largely intact. Forty-five minutes on foot covers it, and it beats downtown Wilmington by a mile.

    Delaware St, New Castle DE ↗ (map) Battery Park ↗ (map) Free
  4. 12:30 PM

    12:30 PM: Lunch — Jessop's Tavern

    Colonial tavern on the cobblestones, creaking wood floors, Belgian beer list, and a shepherd's pie that arrives the size of a brick.

    114 Delaware St, New Castle DE ↗ (map) ~$60
  5. 2:00 PM
    Drive 50m — east over the Delaware Memorial Bridge ↗ (map) ($5), then NJ-295 north. Forty miles through New Jersey on the way, with no detour at all. That puts you in Camden around 2:50; the spare 40 minutes absorbs bridge traffic.
  6. 3:30 PM

    3:30 PM: Camden waterfront, New Jersey

    Battleship New Jersey is docked here, and the view back across the Delaware at the Philadelphia skyline is the actual reason to stop. Late afternoon light does the work.

    62 Battleship Pl, Camden NJ ↗ (map) Free · waterfront
  7. 5:00 PM
    Drive 20m — Ben Franklin Bridge ↗ (map) ($5) into Old City. Check in, drop the bags. Hotel: not booked yet
  8. 7:00 PM

    7:00 PM: Dinner — The Plough & the Stars

    Old City Irish pub at Chestnut and 2nd, in an old bank building. Open till 2 a.m., so there's no rush getting there.

    123 Chestnut St, Philadelphia PA ↗ (map) ~$75
  9. 9:00 PM

    9:00 PM: Independence Hall and the Liberty Bell — tonight, not tomorrow

    Both lit after dark and both a short walk from dinner. The Liberty Bell Center is glass-walled, so the bell is visible from the sidewalk at any hour, and Independence Hall's exterior is lit too.

    Do it now. Thursday morning does not exist, and the timed interior tickets wouldn't fit a forty-minute window anyway.

    Liberty Bell Center ↗ (map) Independence Hall ↗ (map) Free

Why DC comes first Washington sits west of the Chesapeake; Delaware, New Jersey and Philadelphia all sit on one line northeast of it. Hitting Delaware before DC would cost four hours of backtracking, and running DC as a day trip from Philly would cost five. The order is set by the map, not by preference.
